## Direction for the Next Phase
This year, we are focusing on stronger iteration loops between prototype and player feedback. We will keep the scope tight and prioritize systems that produce high replay value.

## Main Priorities
- Strengthen multiplayer readability in our current game prototypes.
- Ship one public demo with a complete onboarding flow.
- Improve build stability and QA checklists for every milestone.

## Communication Plan
We will share monthly progress updates through Notice and deeper process notes through Devlog.
